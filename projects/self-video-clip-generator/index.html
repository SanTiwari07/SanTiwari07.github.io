Self Video Clip Generator

Automatically cut Reels and Shorts out of long YouTube videos

Built by Sanskar Tiwari (santiwari07)

Source on GitHub ↗

An AI tool that automatically extracts and generates Reels and Shorts from long-form YouTube videos using a fully Python-based pipeline.

Give it a long video and it finds the strongest moments and exports them as short vertical clips ready to post.

What it does

  • End-to-end Python pipeline
  • Finds and cuts highlight segments automatically
  • Exports vertical clips for Reels and Shorts

Built with

  • Python
  • AI
  • Video processing
