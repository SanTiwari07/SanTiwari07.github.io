UniFit Health

AI wellness app for college students powered by Gemini multimodal AI

Built by Sanskar Tiwari (santiwari07)

Live demo ↗ Source on GitHub ↗

UniFit Health is an AI-powered health and wellness tracker for college students that combines water tracking, calorie counting, steps, gym logging, posture analysis and personalised insights.

It uses Gemini multimodal AI to analyse food photos, posture images and daily activity, and turns them into clear recommendations.

What it does

  • MealScan: calories, macros and advice from a food photo
  • PainScan: posture and muscle-strain analysis from a photo
  • HydraAI hydration tracking that adapts to activity
  • Google sign-in and a personalised plan at onboarding

Built with

  • TypeScript
  • Gemini
  • Google AI Studio
  • Vercel
