Arivo

AI finance controller that keeps AI inside deterministic accounting rules

Built by Sanskar Tiwari (santiwari07)

Live demo ↗ Watch video ↗ Source on GitHub ↗

Arivo (repository: Aviro) is an invariant-governed AI finance controller. It puts deterministic accounting rules around probabilistic AI, so no money moves without authorisation and nothing is auto-matched by mistake.

“Know where every rupee went, or know exactly why you don't.” In Arivo, AI investigates, rules verify, controls protect and humans resolve ambiguity.

In the flagship safety scenario, Gemini recommends matching a ₹6,00,000 payment with 97% confidence, and Arivo's Control Gate vetoes the match and holds the money in review because the evidence does not meet the rules.

What it does

  • Guarantees zero unauthorised capital movement and zero false auto-matches
  • Deterministic waterfall matching with a Control Gate that can veto AI recommendations
  • Evidence drawer and a grounded “Ask Arivo” copilot
  • Benchmark suite and backend tests included

Built with

  • Python
  • Gemini
  • React
